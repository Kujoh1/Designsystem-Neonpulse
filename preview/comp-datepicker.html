How it behaves
  • From 768 px the calendar opens as a popover under the field; below it becomes a bottom sheet with a grab handle and 44 px days.
  • Keyboard: arrows move by day and week, Home / End by week, PageUp / PageDown by month (Shift: year), Esc closes.
  • Typing works too: separators are added while you type, invalid text sets aria-invalid.
  • The script reads data-min / data-max as ISO dates, today or +30d / -18y.
